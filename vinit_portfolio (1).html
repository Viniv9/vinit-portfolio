About

The marketer who never stopped asking why.

I didn't start in tech. I started in data tables, client dashboards, and the messy work of figuring out why a loyalty campaign underperformed for one segment while it flew for another. That curiosity — the need to understand the system behind the outcome — has driven every role I've held.

But more than that, I built the reporting infrastructure that tells us where customers drop off, what keeps them engaged, and what a loyalty program needs to do differently next month. That's not just marketing — that's product thinking applied to retention.

I'm now actively looking to take these skills — campaign analytics, CRM operations, data-informed decision-making — into a tech-forward team where marketing is a growth engine, not an afterthought.
