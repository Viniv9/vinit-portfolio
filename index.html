05 / Paid Advertising · Side Project
How I Got Humbled, Went to School, and Delivered 6× ROI
Rosemount International Preschool — Independent Campaign

I'll be honest: I took this on thinking it would be an effortless side hustle. A few local campaigns, some clicks, money on autopilot. Reality humbled me fast.

The first two weeks — clicks climbing, admissions completely flat. We were burning budget to buy attention that went nowhere. So I stepped away from the dashboard and walked into the school.

Watching teachers with toddlers, listening to parents at the front desk — the flaw became obvious. We were running dry, generic ads that treated parents like data points, ignoring the enormous emotional weight behind choosing a child's very first school.

I tore down the strategy and rebuilt from scratch.

What went wrong
Generic creatives. Broad targeting. No emotional resonance. Clicks arrived — but parents choosing a preschool for their toddler need to feel the school, not just see an ad. We had skipped the most important variable entirely.
The pivot
Visited the school in person. Observed classrooms, spoke with parents. Then broke the audience into distinct parent segments, ran sharp A/B tests on messaging and creatives, and overhauled everything to showcase genuine safety, warmth, and real school life — not stock photos and bullet points.
What I took away
Algorithms cannot fix what empathy fails to understand. You cannot run high-performing ads from behind a laptop if you never take the time to truly know the people clicking them. That lesson has shaped every campaign I've run since.
40 admissions · 6× ROAS · Audience segmentation + A/B messaging overhaul
